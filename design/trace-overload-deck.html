Trace — the overload deck

Revision 6. The Flight Deck screens, plus the thing that was missing: a colour system that answers am I actually progressively overloading? per movement, not per session.

Colour here never means good or bad. It means how much Trace actually knows. That is the whole difference between this and every other tracker’s green-arrow-everywhere dashboard, where 89% of sets get marked as improvement and the signal is worth nothing.

Confirmed

Eight or more sessions and the smoothed trend is up. This is Trace saying you got stronger and being willing to be wrong about it.

Building

You are beating previous bests, but under eight sessions. Every one is a true fact about that day. None of them is a trend.

Held

Enough sessions, trend flat. A verdict too — just not the one anyone wants. Six weeks flat on a squat is information.

Declining

Enough sessions, trend down. Shown plainly rather than buried, because the alternative is finding out much later.

Not yet

Too few sessions, too few reps for the maths, or a gap long enough that the old data no longer describes you.

Progressive overload

Four screens. The board answers the question at a glance; the other three show the working.

Activities

66 activities across five families, indoor and outdoor — and the Indian ones nobody else carries.

What this is not

The restraint is the product. Worth stating plainly, because it is the part that is easy to lose.

No movement turns green from one good session. No verdict survives a four-week gap. A set logged at sixteen reps gets no estimate at all, because the formula stops meaning anything past twelve and a number that means nothing is worse than a blank. Every threshold here — eight sessions, twelve reps, four weeks — is written down in the constants table with an honest label saying whether evidence, convention or judgement put it there.

Screens are mockups. The strength engine, the session summary and the route are implemented and verified against real logged sets; the overload board and the expanded catalogue are not built yet.
