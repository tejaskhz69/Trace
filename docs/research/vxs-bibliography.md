## The only formula stated on the page

    1RM = w · (1 + r/30)          [Epley, ref 1-01]

Note what VXS says about its own source: **"technical pamphlet, not
peer-reviewed"**. They label it honestly and then cite LeSuer 1997 for the
validation (r = 0.95–0.97). That is the pattern worth copying — not the value.
